## Re: An Open Letter Against Apple's Privacy-Invasive Content Scanning Technology

I've sorta been an Apple fanboy since the 2010s or so. It's not that long a time a time, but I've loved my MacBooks, iPads, iPhones, and iWatches.

But I feel like in recent times the Apple I thought I knew (and loved) has changed, and not in a way I'd like. It all started w/ the App Store, and how their policies have negatively impacted all parties in this ecosystem -- including themselves. Most recently, it's been their [Expanded Protections for Children](https://www.apple.com/child-safety/) features.

To be clear, CSAM - Child Sexual Abuse Material scanning in iCloud Photos currently applies in the United States and not Singapore. And the very idea of CSAM as a thing, is abhorent.

At the same time, I use iCloud Photos, and I don't really want a company to one day determine that a CSAM-scanning algorithm must run on my own iPhone.

I'd like a say, please. So my own small way of protesting is not to personally use ny Apple hardware pre-installed with iOS 15, iPadOS 15, watchOS 8, and macOS Monterey; i.e., any existing devices I own/control, as well as any new devices come September (and later).

I've also signed [An Open Letter Against Apple's Privacy-Invasive Content Scanning Technology](https://appleprivacyletter.com/) -- you'll need a GitHub account. I'm signatory #3784, and at this point I'm seeing 8436 signatures: you could be #8437.
