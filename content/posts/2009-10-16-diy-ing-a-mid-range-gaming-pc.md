About a year ago, I led myself to believe that I'd find the time to play Fallout 3 on this new gaming PC that I'd build. Here are the parts that I'd picked, with my dear friend Eddie's help, circa December 2008:

|            |Part                                            |Price |
|------------|------------------------------------------------|------|
|GPU         |Gainward 4850 512MB                             |244   |
|CPU         |Intel Core 2 Duo E8400                          |474   |
|MotherBoard |Gigabyte GA-EP45-UD3P                           |0     |
|RAM         |Corsair PC2-6400 DDR2-800 (2x2GB)               |128   |
|HDD         |WD Caviar Black 640GB                           |138   |
|Power Supply|Vantec 500N ION2+500W                           |75    |
|Case        |3R System K400                                  |59    |

The total is SGD 1118, excluding an optical drive, monitor, keyboard, mouse and speakers. I didn't buy it in the end, and I didn't find the time to play much of anything, so it turned out to be a good decision.
