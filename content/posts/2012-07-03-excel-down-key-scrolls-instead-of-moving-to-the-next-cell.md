---
title: Down key scrolls instead of moving to the next cell in Excel
date: 2012-07-03T16:11:14+00:00
---

Learnt something new today. I was using Excel with my keyboard, and was puzzled why pressing the down key scrolled instead of moving (down) to the next cell as I'd expected.

So I [Googled](https://www.google.com.sg/search?q=excel+down+key+scrolls+instead+of+next+cell+down) and found this [excellent post](http://www.computerhope.com/issues/ch001217.htm). It turns out I'd inadvertently turned on Scroll Lock.
