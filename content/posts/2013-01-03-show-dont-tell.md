title: Show, don't tell
date: 2013-01-03T07:07:33+00:00
---

"Show, don't tell" is an ad about some English enrichment class. So instead of saying that John is smart, _show_ how smart he is: John asks thoughtful and insightful questions, solves problems others take awhile to delve into, and so on and so forth.

Bonus: George R. R. Martin's Game of Thrones version: "Words are wind".
