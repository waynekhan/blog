---
title: RDP copy-paste is not working
date: 2017-02-07T09:54:18+00:00
---

Fixing this issue is pretty straightforward and involves a few simple steps.

1. Load up task manager (right click taskbar and select Task Manager)
1. Go to the Processes Tab
1. Select rdpclip.exe
1. Click End Process
1. Go to the Application Tab
1. Click New Process
1. Type rdpclip
1. Click Ok
