---
title: Multiple displays on Windows 10
date: 2017-07-28T02:38:58+00:00
---

I use my 23" monitor as the main display when paired with my 14" Windows laptop (HP EliteBook 840 G3). 150% display scaling is fine with 23" of glorious, screen real estate, but [eye-achingly small](http://www.infoworld.com/article/2952506/microsoft-windows/high-resolution-displays-reveal-windows-10-blind-spot.html) on just 14"; i.e., when I go for meetings. As a result, I'd toggle between 100% and 150% display scaling, and there's the added hassle of having to re-login for this change to take effect.

Today I learned that you can configure separate settings for each display when using"Extend these displays" rather than "Duplicate these displays". My thanks to [this other article](https://www.thurrott.com/windows/windows-10/4597/windows-10-feature-focus-display-scaling), saved me a couple of minutes each and every time!
