## New iPad Pro

2 screen sizes (11-inch, 13-inch). "Ultra Retina XDR" displays.

* Thickness: 5.3 (and 5.1) mm. (Ed: How come the larger device is thinner?)
* Weight: .98 (and 1.28) lb.

2 colors (silver, space black).

OLED tech for colour and luminance; 2 OLED panels, and so "Tandem OLED".

1000 nits of brightness for SDR/HDR content.

Nano-texture glass option. (Ed: I want this.)

Graphite and copper, for improved thermal performance.

Front-facing camera shifted to the landscape edge, just like iPad Air.

999 (and 1299) USD for 256 GB of storage.

## New iPad Air

Now in 2 screen sizes (11-inch, 13-inch). The displays are identical.

Interesting titbit about iPad Pro: "... about half the users choose the larger screen". (Ed: Not 65-35, in favour of the smaller screen?)

Front-facing camera shifted to the landscape edge.

"Center Stage" camera; i.e., it tries to keep everyone in-frame.

What about the keyboard again?

Landscape stereo (i.e., TWO {2}) speakers.

4 colors (blue, purple, starlight, & space grey).

100% recycled aluminium enclosure.

M2 SoC. These percentages probably relative to its predecessor:
  * CPU: +15%;
  * GPU: +25%;
  * NPU (also known as an NPU, or `ane`): +40%;
  * "Faster memory bandwidth". (Ed: Perhaps she meant _more_ memory bandwidth?)
  * Overall: +50% (Ed: Those numbers don't seem to match up.)

"3x faster performance" than A14-based iPad Air.

Now supports Apple Pencil hover. Which Pencil(s) does it support?

599 (and 799) USD for 256 GB of storage.
