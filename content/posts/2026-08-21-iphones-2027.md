I was listening to John Gruber/Chance Miller on [The Talk Show](https://daringfireball.net/thetalkshow/2026/08/10/ep-454) talking about iPhone pricing come September. I promptly stopped listening to write this post comprised of unoriginal thinking. I still find it still helps me clarify my own plans.

As a recap, here are the iPhones 17 lineup pricing in USD from _last September_, effectively ancient history now:

![iPhones 17 lineup](/2025-09-10-iphones-17-lineup.png)

Screenshot from [an earlier post of mine](/posts/2025-09-10-apple-awe-dropping-event/). I don't know that the numbers above will hold for their upcoming lineup [in view of RAMageddon](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/may/06/ramageddon-cheap-phones-laptops-macbooks-ps5s-ai-chips). Apple has already [raised prices on its devices, including Macs, iPads, TV -- the device, HomePods, and Vision Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/25/apple-just-increased-prices/) with the notable exception of iPhone, of course.

To this end, I created a table with separate columns for best-/worst-case scenario pricing. I hope I was overly-pessimistic, but time will tell. Needless to say, I'm just a fan of the products is all.

Come September, 3 new iPhones, namely the folding iPhone, and new and improved Pro and Air models. Last year's non-Pro and e phones will be available, but the "vanilla" and "e" line won't be available until January, and so a big change in what was once an annual schedule:

|                      | Released | Optimist | Pessimist | Memo                                                                                                   |
|----------------------|----------|----------|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| iPhone "Fold" (2027) | Sep '26  | 1800     | 2100      | Foldable screens are expensive.                                                                        |
| iPhone Pro (2027)    | Sep '26  | 1100     | 1300      | New and improved over last year's 17 Pro.                                                              |
| iPhone Air (2027)    | Sep '26  | 1000     | 1150      | New and improved over last year's Air, which I find both groundbreaking and immature at the same time. |
| iPhone 17            | Sep '25  | 700      | 850       |                                                                                                        |
| iPhone 17e           | Mar '26  | 500      | 600       |                                                                                                        |

(This does makes for a temporary hole in the lineup between USD 700 and 1000 {optimist}, or USD 850 1150 {pessimist} until 2027.)
